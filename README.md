## About the project

EvolvingAtlas is a web-based editor for building historical and alternative geopolitical maps. Users can draw borders, assign territory to nations, place historically relevant locations, trace routes and campaigns, and record how a world changes from one year to another — including dates BC/BCE.

It is intended for history enthusiasts, educators, students, strategists and storytellers.

**The maps are authored by users, not automatically generated by AI.**

## What you can do

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 🗺️ Base maps | Start from a blank geographic map or a natural, satellite-inspired map. |
| ✏️ Borders & territories | Draw borders, split land into provinces and assign territories to nations. |
| 🕰️ Historical timeline | Date territorial changes and move through years to explore different moments in history. |
| 📍 Places & events | Mark cities, capitals, ports, fortresses, religious sites and battle locations. |
| ➡️ Routes & campaigns | Illustrate land or sea routes, military movements and other journeys. |
| 🖼️ Export | Export a composed map as an image from an account. |

> Feature availability and plan limits may change as the product evolves. The live product is the source of truth.
